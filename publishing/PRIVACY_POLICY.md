# LumaStack Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 17 September 2026

LumaStack processes photos on your Android device to combine them into one image. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your photos or personal information. It does not use analytics, advertising, accounts, or a remote processing service.

When you capture a series, the device's camera app creates temporary images for LumaStack. When you choose existing photos, Android's document picker grants access only to the photos you select. LumaStack reads those images locally and saves the finished JPEG in **Pictures/LumaStack**. Temporary capture files may remain in the app's cache until Android clears the cache or you clear the app's storage.

LumaStack does not request Internet access. The camera app and Android document picker are separate system components and may follow their own policies.

To remove a finished photo, delete it from your Photos or Files app. To remove temporary app files, clear LumaStack's app storage or uninstall the app.
