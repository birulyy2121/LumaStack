# Google Play listing draft

**App name:** LumaStack

**Short description:** Stack photos into one polished image, entirely on your device.

**Full description:**

Turn a series of photos into one finished image with LumaStack. Choose how many shots to use, take them with your camera, or select photos you already have. LumaStack aligns the frames, blends exposure detail, adds a gentle color grade, and saves the result to your Pictures folder.

Everything runs on your device. No account, upload, subscription, or internet connection is needed.

For best results, photograph a still scene from one position, ideally with different exposures. Output size is up to 2048 pixels on the longest side. Moving subjects or large camera shifts may cause artifacts.

**Category:** Photography

**Suggested tags:** photo editor, photography

**Data safety draft:** No user data collected or shared by the app. Verify this against the final release build and any future dependencies before submitting the Play Console form.

**Required before submission:** Real app screenshots from a tested device, a publicly accessible privacy-policy URL, support email, content rating, target audience, Data safety form, and signed AAB.
