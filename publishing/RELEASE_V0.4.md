# LumaStack Preview 0.4 — Computational Inspector

## What’s new

- Tap anywhere in the built-in viewfinder to focus and meter exposure.
- Choose quick 3x, 5x, 8x, or 12x stack presets.
- LumaStack now selects a sharp, balanced reference frame automatically.
- Coarse-to-fine alignment handles more handheld movement.
- Exposure fusion now considers well-exposedness, saturation, and local contrast.
- Motion-aware weighting reduces ghosts around moving subjects.
- The finished-image inspector supports pinch zoom, panning, reference comparison, alignment statistics, and native sharing.
- Filmic highlight roll-off and skin-aware saturation keep the grade natural.
- Both Android 6–9 and Android 10+ builds include the upgrade.

## Choose the correct APK

- **Android 6, 7, 8, or 9:** `LumaStack-Android6-9.apk`
- **Android 10 or newer:** `LumaStack-Android10-plus.apk`

These are debug-signed preview builds. If Android reports an update or signature conflict, uninstall the older preview first.
